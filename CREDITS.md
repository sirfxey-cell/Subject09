# Credits

## Sound effects

Most sound effects in `sfx/` come from **"Oculus Audio Pack 01"** by Oculus VR, LLC
(https://developers.meta.com/vr/downloads/package/oculus-audio-pack-1/), licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Files were renamed, cut to length and
converted to mono MP3 for this game.

Creature growls, roars, grunts and breaths (`growl*`, `roar*`, `grunt*`, `breath*`) are by
**rubberduck** from OpenGameArt ("80 CC0 creature SFX" and "80 CC0 RPG SFX"), released under
[CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

`bigroar.mp3` is a new mix made for this game: an Oculus Audio Pack giant scream layered with
rubberduck roars and growls, pitched down, mixed together and faded out. `rumble1.mp3` is a cut of
the Oculus Audio Pack subway sound.
