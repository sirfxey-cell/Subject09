SUBJECT 09

Swing through the dark like a gorilla. Stay inside the safe zone, or don’t.

  • Push off the ground and walls with your controllers to move.
  • Use the terminal in the lobby to set your name, color, or join a private code.
  • Vote for a level, then step through a portal: Classic, Hide & Seek, or Be the Monster.
  • If you’re caught, you watch everyone else on a wall of screens.
Online play connects the people who have this page open. Screen: drag to look, WASD to walk, L lamp, click the terminal.
