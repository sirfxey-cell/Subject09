Subject 09 Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Subject 09 is a multiplayer VR horror game. This page explains what the game does with your information.

What we do not collect

Subject 09 has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics. We do not ask for your real name, email, location or payment details, and we do not sell or share any personal information.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your settings on your own device (in your browser's local storage): your in-game name, color, cosmetics, coins, friends list, volume and graphics settings. This data stays on your device. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's or browser's site data.

Multiplayer

When you join a public or private server, the game connects directly to other players (peer to peer) using public relay servers to find them. Other players in your room receive what they need to see you in the game: your in-game name, color, cosmetics, and the positions of your head and hands. Like any internet connection, the people and relay servers you connect to can see your IP address.

Voice chat and microphone

If you allow microphone access, your voice is sent live to the other players in your room so they can hear you. Voice is not recorded or stored by the game. You can turn your microphone off at any time on the lobby computer, and you can decline microphone access entirely and still play.

Moderation

Moderators can remove (kick) or silence players in a room. A random device ID stored on your device is used so moderation and friends work across name changes. It is not linked to your identity.

Children

Subject 09 contains intense horror, blood and gore and is not intended for children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/sirfxey-cell/Subject09.
